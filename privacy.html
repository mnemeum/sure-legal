SURE privacy policy

Mnemeum · Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy covers the SURE evaluation app provided by Mnemeum on Atlassian Forge, installed as Northstar Judge Lab. It also covers the allocation, Ahead (formerly Sakini), and Parity (formerly Migration) sandboxes available in that installation. Privacy and support contact: mnemeum@gmail.com.

The release includes automated Atlassian personal-data reporting and saved-record redaction. Privacy-redacted runs are visibly read-only and cannot authorize repairs. Reporting is periodic, so account closure does not immediately erase app records.

Information processed

SURE processes Atlassian account IDs to separate users’ saved work, check ownership and permissions, attribute changes, and record approvals. Saved records may include another user’s account ID, display name, account type, assignee values, and profile fields returned in Jira history. Administrator and app-account IDs can also be present. The app does not ask for your Atlassian password or API token.

Records include site, project and issue identifiers; synthetic issue fields; before-and-after values; native changelogs; comments; timestamps; selected change windows and actors; missions; proposals; approvals; results; holds and release audits; Rovo action receipts; recovery and background-job state; saved previous runs; and evidence artifacts. Jira history copies can include changes beyond the fields SURE repairs. Free-text comments and sandbox inputs can contain personal information if you enter it. Use fictional information for this evaluation.

Operational logs include action, run and job identifiers, timings, status, and API and storage counters. Some sandbox errors are logged as truncated text. Logs and error text may be linkable to a person. They are not guaranteed anonymous.

Purpose and use

We use these records to operate the requested demonstrations, check permissions and playground custody, prepare reviews, perform approved synthetic changes and repairs, retain evidence, reconcile interruptions, and diagnose faults. SURE writes only within its isolated synthetic Jira playground; customer issues remain outside its repair workflow.

We do not sell personal information. The app has no advertising, marketing database, or third-party analytics integration.

Atlassian hosting, Forge LLM and Rovo

App records are stored in Atlassian Forge hosted Key-Value Store and Custom Entity Store. Background work uses Forge queues. Native issues, comments and history are stored in Jira on the installing site. The app declares no remote backend or external network egress destination and has no developer-operated external AI integration.

SURE uses Forge LLM to generate explanations from bounded saved evidence. That evidence can include issue keys, actor IDs and names, selected changes, held fields and governance facts. Generated text, validation results and attempt metadata can be saved with the run. Rovo receives the chat you provide and can call SURE actions to read saved evidence, prepare a human review, or perform explicitly requested faulty synthetic triage. Rovo does not approve SURE repairs or release holds. Atlassian operates these services, including its AI providers, under its own platform policies. The absence of an app-declared external destination does not mean Atlassian uses no subprocessors.

Forge hosted storage follows Atlassian’s applicable hosting and residency arrangements. This is not a promise that all logs, AI processing, support records and downloaded files stay in a particular country. See Atlassian’s hosted-storage lifecycle and Atlassian’s privacy policy.

Access and downloaded copies

Saved app work is scoped to the signed-in user and installation. Jira’s own permissions control access to the synthetic issues and their history. Other authorized site users may therefore see that Jira content. Authorized app developers may access diagnostic logs when the site’s log-sharing settings permit.

Evidence exports contain identifying information, including account IDs, names when present, site and issue links, and saved activity or entered text. An integrity hash does not anonymize an export. Downloaded files are held on your device and are outside the app’s deletion controls. If you send us an export or support request, we receive the information you send.

Retention and account closure

The current release has no automatic age-based expiration of saved runs, receipts, archives, audit records or sandbox artifacts. Starting a new run retains previous evidence. Execution timeouts are not deletion timers.

An hourly Forge task inventories stored Atlassian account IDs and reports them to Atlassian’s Personal data reporting API, with the oldest recorded retrieval date. The default per-account reporting period is seven days; the app follows a different Cycle-Period supplied by Atlassian. Rate limits, interrupted work and processing progress are saved for later retries. Historical records whose retrieval date was not tracked use a conservative old date rather than being presented as newly retrieved.

When Atlassian reports an account as closed, the app deletes that account’s own saved namespace and artifacts, and removes linked account identifiers, cached profile fields, identifying text and authored comment bodies from other affected app records. This includes saved snapshots, native-history copies, governance/audit records and Rovo receipts. Affected runs are marked privacy-redacted, read-only; original integrity receipts are invalidated and separate redacted-snapshot checks are kept. These runs cannot be used for repair. Unaffected runs remain unchanged. This is a deliberate privacy exception to the normal guarantee that saved evidence is immutable.

When Atlassian reports updated personal data, the app drops cached profile fields and linked old display labels. Saved evidence changed by this process is also marked privacy-redacted and read-only. Processing uses resumable checkpoints and temporarily locks app access and mutations while in progress. With the hourly schedule, that maintenance lock can last roughly an hour or longer for a larger inventory or after an error. Minimal hashed account suppression markers are retained to prevent erased account data from being stored again; these are pseudonymous safeguards, not a claim of anonymous data.

This process changes app-controlled Forge records. It does not delete native Jira issues, comments or history, Atlassian platform logs or queue infrastructure, downloaded exports, or support copies outside those records. Those have separate controls and lifecycles. Free text that cannot be linked to an account may require a specific deletion request.

Optional playground cleanup requires separate confirmation and deletes that run’s owned Jira issues. It does not erase all app records, project configuration, logs, exports or support copies. Uninstallation is subject to Atlassian’s hosted-storage retention and recovery lifecycle and does not immediately erase every copy. Jira data, platform logs and downloaded files have separate lifecycles.

Your choices and requests

Use synthetic information, review proposals before approval, and inspect exports before sharing them. A site administrator can uninstall the app. Contact mnemeum@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we process. Include the relevant site and run where possible; do not send passwords or tokens. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with your site administrator.
