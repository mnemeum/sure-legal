SURE privacy policy

Mnemeum · Last updated November 18, 2026

SURE is Mnemeum's evaluation app on Atlassian Forge. Privacy and support contact: mnemeum@gmail.com.

SURE reads Jira activity to learn and watch automation and app-account behaviour, rather than model people's behaviour. People's account details and edits can still appear in the evidence; selected or SURE-managed projects are not anonymous.

Information and use

SURE reads selected projects, issue fields and history, account profiles and Jira configuration to learn patterns, explain signals, review proposed repairs and verify outcomes. Saved records include site, project and issue identifiers; account IDs, names and assignee values; field observations and change measurements; learned and declared rules; proof counts and charts; incidents; approvals; repair and verification receipts; and recovery progress. Settings, Check-ins, review answers and human decisions can include the person, time and entered reason.

For standard summary, description and environment fields, Sense's compact observations retain text-presence and change measurements instead of full contents. Other values, including labels and custom fields, can contain personal information; repair and playground records can retain fuller native history, comments and entered text. Operational logs can contain run and job identifiers, timings, statuses, API and storage counters, AI usage and error details that may be linkable to a person.

When enabled, goal context reads linked Jira dependencies and Teamwork Graph goal identifiers, names and links with the viewer's access, using a short in-memory cache. Incident cause lookup saves reduced configuration-audit context, including configuration names, changed values and timing, with a generic administrator marker instead of the audit author's identity. A suggested cause is not proof of who caused an incident.

Repairs and the demo

Sense can repair priority, assignee and status in real projects an administrator has explicitly marked SURE-managed, after a person approves the exact repair plan and Jira permission, native-history and current-value checks pass. Later edits are protected by these checks. The original playground limits its writes to its owned synthetic issues.

Ensure creates labelled synthetic Jira test issues in the managed project and saves test identifiers, expected results, observations and outcomes. Its finish step marks those issues retained rather than deleting them.

The demo combines labelled simulated past history with real sample tickets and bounded generated activity on the installing site. It saves the owner, consent, source labels, progress and native receipts, and can read human edits to protect later decisions. In retained-project mode, Stop ends further demo work but leaves the sample project and tickets in Jira; an administrator can delete the project in Jira Project settings. Where exclusive-project cleanup is offered, confirmed removal checks ownership and refuses to delete a project containing other data.

Hosting, Forge LLM and Rovo

App records use Atlassian Forge hosted Key-Value Store and Custom Entity Store, with Forge queues for background work; native issues, comments and history remain in Jira. This build declares no remote backend or external network destination.

Forge LLM explanation requests can include issue keys, actor IDs and names, selected field values and changes, learned or declared rules, proof counts, rates, thresholds and possible-cause context. If expected-behaviour drafting is enabled, it sends your description and available labels and priority names to Forge LLM. Checked explanations and attempt or usage metadata are saved; some playground explanation paths also retain raw model responses and tool-call output.

Rovo receives your chat and the authorised saved evidence returned by SURE actions. The catch-and-repair recap is assembled from saved incident, repair and verification receipts without another model call in that action; Rovo's chat response is separate AI processing. SURE Watch's Rovo actions are read-only; the original playground agent can prepare a review or perform explicitly requested synthetic faulty triage, but cannot approve repairs or release holds.

Atlassian operates these AI services under its privacy policy and subprocessor arrangements; see also Forge LLMs. This policy does not promise zero AI retention or that all processing stays in one country. Forge provides data residency for persistent hosted storage, which does not establish the location of AI processing, logs, support copies or downloads.

Access and downloads

Playground runs are scoped to their initiating user and installation, with explicit incident delegation where supported; Sense uses installation-shared records with owner, administrator and current Jira access checks. Background monitoring uses the configured owner's authorised Jira access, and Jira permissions govern native issues and history. The Rovo recap requires a Jira administrator and, for a demo, the exact demo owner and run.

Evidence exports can include account IDs, names, site and issue links, saved changes and entered text. The administrator-only Export view snapshot masks personal profiles, known personal identities, assignees and unstructured text, but can retain automation identities, issue and project identifiers, behaviour data and checked explanations. A hash verifies integrity, not anonymity; review downloads before sharing because files on your device are outside the app's deletion controls.

Retention and account closure

Sense keeps the active study and the most recent 12 historical studies in full, plus entire older studies with open incidents, unresolved or pending original work, or insufficient evidence of closure. Older closed studies compact into one immutable rolling custody summary containing counts, outcomes, dates and cryptographic commitments, without names, account IDs, issue IDs or entered text. The summary can verify supplied historical receipts, but cannot reconstruct removed records or serve as a backup. Removal and legacy migration run in resumable batches, so cleanup is not immediate; protected unresolved cases can still grow. This study-count policy is not a general age-based expiry for playground runs, other app records, native Jira data, logs or downloads.

An hourly Forge task inventories stored Atlassian account IDs and reports them to Atlassian's Personal data reporting API using recorded retrieval dates. Per-account reporting normally follows a seven-day period or Atlassian's supplied Cycle-Period, with saved checkpoints and retries; closed-account data is not erased immediately or guaranteed within one hour. When Atlassian reports a closed account, SURE deletes that account's saved namespace and removes linked identifying information from affected app-controlled records. Affected runs become privacy-redacted and read-only, their original integrity receipts cannot authorise repairs, and affected sealed Sense records become unusable privacy tombstones. Updated-account reports refresh or remove cached profiles; affected sealed Sense records can also be invalidated. Privacy processing may temporarily block access and changes, and retains minimal hashed suppression markers to prevent closed-account data returning. Those markers are pseudonymous, not anonymous, and free text that cannot be linked to an account may need a specific deletion request.

App deletion and redaction do not erase native Jira issues or history, platform logs, downloaded exports or support copies outside app-controlled records. Uninstallation and storage deletion follow Atlassian's hosted-storage retention and recovery lifecycle, including platform copies the app can no longer read.
